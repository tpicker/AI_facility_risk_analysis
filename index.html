How to use this calculator

For each location, examine all 16 decision factors. Describe a relevant risk, assign its likelihood and impact (1–5), and propose a mitigation strategy. Risk score = likelihood × impact. The heat map shows the frequency of scored factors at each likelihood–impact combination, as in the original workbook. A factor's rank is 1 plus the number of factors with higher scores; tied scores share the same rank.
